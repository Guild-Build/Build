description: Maintains a ledger of every source a document relies on: location (URL or file), the exact claim it supports, the date read, and whether a second source agrees. Refuses to let a number into the text without a ledger row. Use while researching or drafting anything with facts.
---

# Source ledger

Keep a table in the working document, or in SOURCES.md beside it:

| # | Source (URL or path) | Claim it supports | Read on | Second source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- Every figure, date, name and quotation in the draft points at a row number.
- "Second source" is a second independent location that agrees, or "none".
- A row whose source could not be opened is marked "unverified" and the claim is softened or removed.
- When two sources disagree, both rows stay and the text says they disagree.
