# Measured article

Structure, in this order:

1. **Title** that states the finding, not the topic.
2. **First paragraph**: the direct answer in three sentences. A reader who stops here knows the result.
3. **Body**: one section per measurement. Each number appears with the conditions it was measured under and the source row from the source ledger.
4. **How we know**: sample size, instrument, date, what was not measured, and what would change the conclusion.
5. **Cite this**: title, author, date, canonical URL, version.

Rules: no adjective where a number will do; no number without a method; no claim from memory presented as observed. Mark estimates as estimates.
