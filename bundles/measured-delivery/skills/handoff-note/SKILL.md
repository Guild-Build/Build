# Handoff note

Finish every task with exactly this shape, in plain language, no narration of your process:

- **Changed**: the files or behaviour that are different now.
- **Verified**: what you ran and what it showed (command and result).
- **Not verified**: anything you could not run or did not check. Write "nothing" only if that is true.
- **Left dirty**: uncommitted or incomplete work and why, or "nothing".
- **Next**: the single most useful next step and who should take it.

Keep each line under 30 words. A reader who sees only this note must know the full state.
